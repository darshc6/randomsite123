01 / Meet your workspace

Everything on your mind.
Finally, in one place.

The passing thought. The project in progress. The conversation worth remembering. Bring it all into Slate, then let local AI help you find the connections.

A home for your projects, notes, and everything in between.Inside Slate / macOS

Make room for everything.

Create separate workspaces and projects for different parts of your life. Keep documents, notes, links, and recordings together.

Catch the passing thought.

Type it or say it. Quick Capture turns a fleeting idea into a note, then helps title and sort it so you can keep moving.

Ask. Find. Connect.

Search across your project with AI. Rediscover relevant information, explore a question, or use what you know to start something new.

A little help, in context.

Use Slate Assist inside a document to brainstorm, clarify your thinking, and draw on the contexts you choose.

Stay in the conversation.

Record a meeting without an extra bot joining. Get a local AI summary, pull out action items, and ask questions about what was discussed.

Turn talk into next steps.

Find the tasks tucked inside a conversation. Slate brings them into a checklist you can work through, one small step at a time.

03 / Why we're building Slate

An ambitious idea.
A simpler everyday.
More space to do your thing.

We’re building Slate around a simple belief: keeping up with your ideas should feel easier. Less time searching for that note. Less effort turning a conversation into a plan. More room for the work you care about.

It’s an ambitious project, and this public beta is an early step. We’re shaping a workspace that grows with the way people actually think, create, and get things done. Come help us make it better.
